ABOUT US

Hi! We’re Digitsofpi and Kineto, two friends devoted to coding games, experiments, and anything else that sparks our interest!

We mainly code games in Unity, but we got our start on Khan Academy. We still check in and post there sometimes!

Our story

  1. 2021

    Where it started

    We started coding together on Khan Academy in 2021. Digits made his first game earlier that year, and shortly after our partnership began, Kineto made his. Throughout the year, we conceptualized and partly developed two large-scale games: Shop Run and Aero 2000. Although neither was completed, they were an important stepping stone, teaching us key concepts of game development.

  2. 2022

    One account, two coders

    At the end of the school year, we realized something crucial: Kineto’s Khan Academy account was attached to a school he was leaving. If he didn’t back up his projects, his account and all of his programs would be deleted. We merged our Khan Academy accounts, expecting it to be a temporary fix, but it made collaboration so much easier that we still share an account to this day.

  3. 2025

    Contest winners

    We entered and won a contest in 2025, releasing four high-quality games in just weeks. Along the way, we rebuilt Aero 2000 from scratch, four years after we last touched it.

  4. 2025

    Building DK Converter

    Nearly half of Aero 2000’s development time went into its backgrounds. Khan Academy doesn’t have a native way to load images, so we built a converter from scratch, bought this domain, and released DK Converter. A week later, Khan Academy banned all images, despite them having been around for over a decade. It was done in the name of safety, but whether the change was effective is still up for debate.

  5. 2026

    DASH Parkour

    After remaking an old game idea, we decided to finally finish our most ambitious project yet: DASH Parkour, a 2D platformer where Dash has to beat bosses to become the Parkour Champion. When we looked back at our old code, we realized how messy it was and that we’d need to start over. So we scrapped the old version, which was three years of work, and rebuilt DASH Parkour from the ground up in just a couple of months.

  6. Now

    On to Unity

    Because of the image ban, we decided to escape Khan Academy’s constraints and move to Unity. We learned C#, released DASH Parkour 1.1 with multiplayer, rebuilt this website, and are currently working on our first Unity game.
